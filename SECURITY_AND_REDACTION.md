# Security and Redaction

Content-level scans covered passwords, tokens, keys, connection strings, SAS signatures, subscription identifiers, private endpoints, personal data and histories. Findings and dispositions are logged in the controlled evidence record. Public derivatives include `application/ecommerce/settings.PUBLIC.py` (SECRET_KEY and database password moved to environment variables), `configuration/docker-compose-phase65.PUBLIC.yml` (POSTGRES/DB passwords replaced by `${…}` placeholders), `configuration/platforms.PUBLIC.json` (credential-shaped fields templated) and `environment/pgbouncer_runtime_extract.PUBLIC.txt` (authentication lines redacted). Public derivatives were re-scanned for secret material and structurally re-validated where applicable.

A historical source-capture redaction artefact is documented in `ARTEFACT_PROVENANCE.md`: an earlier capture of `commerce/views.py` was made non-compiling by a redactor replacing a variable reference. **That statement does not apply to the current public `application/commerce/views.py`**, which is a separately reviewed/recovered materialisation that byte-compiles and was exercised during the independent v365 audit. The current/public source-recovery boundary is documented under `application/source-recovery/` and `REPOSITORY_CLOSURE_V365.md`.

The public fixture is a privacy-safe derivative, not the original historical database. It contains 71 objects across the 15-model application schema; 17 user/account objects use synthetic identifying values and unusable passwords. See `application/FIXTURE_PUBLIC_PROVENANCE_V365.md`.

No shell/PowerShell histories, invoices, payment data, private keys or provider credentials are intentionally published. Sensitive original materials remain outside the public repository even when their integrity/provenance is recorded by hash in controlled evidence.
